01 / PROFILE

A practical mindset.
Curiosity with direction.

I am a student and developer in training based in El Salvador. I enjoy building web interfaces, solving technical problems and understanding how software interacts with operating systems, networks and hardware.

My work ranges from websites and collaborative prototypes to Linux utilities, streaming interfaces and Arduino experiments. I care about making projects understandable, maintainable and useful — not just visually impressive.

I learn new tools as projects require them and use AI as a development aid, while keeping responsibility for reviewing, testing and validating the result. I value clear communication, teamwork and steady improvement.
