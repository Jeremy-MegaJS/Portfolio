Privacy &
performance.

Last reviewed: November 2026

Data collection

This portfolio does not include an account system, contact form, analytics platform or first-party advertising tracker. The site itself does not ask visitors to submit personal information. If you contact me by email, your email provider processes that communication.

External services and links

The site may load fonts from Google Fonts and links to third-party services such as GitHub, LinkedIn and project demos. Those services have their own privacy policies and may receive technical information when you visit them. External links open outside this portfolio.

Cookies and local storage

The portfolio uses browser local storage only to remember your language preference when available. It does not use that preference to identify you. You can clear it through your browser settings.

Performance and accessibility

The site uses lightweight HTML, CSS and JavaScript without a frontend framework. Decorative motion is reduced when your operating system requests reduced motion. Layouts adapt to smaller screens, and essential content remains available without animation.

Project demonstrations

Some linked projects are academic prototypes or demonstrations. Their descriptions identify relevant limitations; they should not be treated as production services, security products, identity verification systems or live operational dashboards unless explicitly stated.
